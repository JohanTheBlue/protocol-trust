# Alpha Playtest — v0.18

Ask 3–5 people who have not seen the project to play the 12-email Quick Demo with no prior explanation.

Record:

1. Did the three practice emails make the three choices clear? (Yes / No)
2. Was anything unclear in the rulebook or Link Check? (What?)
3. Were any decisions too obvious or unfair? (Which email reference?)
4. Did Operations and Security feel connected to the choices? (1–5)
5. Would you continue to another shift or the full 60-email game? (Yes / No)

Track: completion, any mistakes, end scores, and points where players got stuck. Prefer revising unclear cases before adding more mechanics.

**Known limitations:** static simulated scan outcomes; a small set of adapted PhishingPot cases; no save/resume or backend. Scores are a simplified game design, not a validated real-world risk measure.
